ScrollPen - draw on any web page or PDF in Google Chrome
=========================================================

Your ink stays on the page when you scroll, and it's still there when you come back.
Everything stays on your computer.


INSTALL (about 2 minutes)
-------------------------

1. Unzip the file you received:
   right-click the .zip file > "Extract All..." > Extract.

2. Move the "ScrollPen" folder somewhere it can stay for good,
   for example your Documents folder.
   Chrome runs ScrollPen from this folder: if you delete or move it later,
   ScrollPen stops working (and your saved ink is lost).

3. Open Google Chrome, type   chrome://extensions   in the address bar and press Enter.

4. Turn on "Developer mode" (the switch in the top-right corner).

5. Click "Load unpacked" (top-left), open the "ScrollPen" folder
   (the one with this file in it) and click "Select Folder".

6. ScrollPen now appears in the list. To keep its button handy:
   click the puzzle-piece icon to the right of Chrome's address bar,
   then click the pin next to "ScrollPen".

7. Optional, for PDFs and pictures saved on your computer:
   on the chrome://extensions page, click "Details" under ScrollPen and
   turn on "Allow access to file URLs".


USE IT
------

- Open any website and click the ScrollPen button (or press Alt+Shift+D).
  A toolbar appears on the right. Draw!
- Scroll with the mouse wheel as usual; the ink moves with the page.
- Press Esc (or the arrow button at the top of the toolbar) to use the page
  normally again. Click the X on the toolbar to close it; your ink stays.
- Hold Caps Lock to click a link without leaving the pen.
- Handy keys while drawing:
    P pen   H highlighter   L laser   E eraser   T text
    I line   A arrow   R rectangle   O ellipse
    W whiteboard   K blackboard   1-9 colours   [ ] size
    Ctrl+Z undo   Ctrl+Y redo   Delete clear the page
- Settings and the list of pages with saved ink: right-click the ScrollPen
  button > "Options".

ScrollPen can't draw on Chrome's own pages (like chrome://extensions or the
Chrome Web Store). PDF links open in ScrollPen's own PDF viewer so you can draw
on them.


GOOD TO KNOW
------------

- Chrome may sometimes show a message about "developer mode extensions".
  That's normal for extensions installed this way; you can close it.
- ScrollPen doesn't update itself. When you get a newer version:
  unzip it, copy everything from its "ScrollPen" folder into your existing
  "ScrollPen" folder (replace the files), then on chrome://extensions click
  the round reload arrow on ScrollPen. Your saved ink is kept.
- To remove ScrollPen: on chrome://extensions click "Remove" under ScrollPen,
  then delete the folder. This also deletes your saved ink.

Privacy: ScrollPen has no account, no servers and no tracking, and sends
nothing anywhere. Your ink is saved only on your computer.
