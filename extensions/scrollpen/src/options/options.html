ScrollPen

Draw on any web page or PDF in Chrome. Click the ScrollPen button in the toolbar, or press Alt+Shift+D.

Welcome! Start in three steps

  1. Click the puzzle-piece icon to the right of Chrome's address bar and pin ScrollPen.
  2. Open any web page and click the ScrollPen button (or press Alt+Shift+D).
  3. Draw. Scroll as usual: your ink stays on the page. Press Esc to use the page normally again.

Optional, for PDFs and pictures saved on your computer: on chrome://extensions, open ScrollPen's Details and turn on Allow access to file URLs.

    While drawing

    Hold to click through
    Hold this key to click links or type on the page without leaving the pen.
    P pen · H highlighter · L laser · E eraser · I line · A arrow · R rectangle · O ellipse · T text · W whiteboard · K blackboard · 1-9 colours · [ ] size · Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y undo / redo · Ctrl+↑/↓ nudge ink · Delete clear · Esc stop drawing. Hold Shift while drawing a shape for straight lines, squares and circles. In text: Enter new line, Ctrl+Enter or Esc to finish. The mouse wheel keeps scrolling the page underneath.

    Keyboard shortcuts

    Shortcuts are set in Chrome. You can also make them work while Chrome isn't the active window.

    Toolbar and PDFs

    Show the toolbar on every page
    It opens ready to use; the page works normally until you pick a tool.
    Toolbar position
    Drag the toolbar by its dots to move it.
    Open PDFs in ScrollPen
    Chrome's own PDF viewer doesn't let extensions draw on it. ScrollPen's viewer shows the PDF on this computer and lets you draw on it.

    Your data

    Save my ink on this computer

    Where it is kept: in Chrome's storage for ScrollPen, in this Chrome profile, on this computer only. It is not synced to your Google account or to any other device, and removing ScrollPen deletes all of it.

    Never sent anywhere. ScrollPen has no servers, no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. It does not read or keep the content of the pages you visit.

    Screenshots are taken only when you click the camera button or use its shortcut, and go to your Downloads folder.

    PDFs: to show a PDF you open, ScrollPen's viewer loads it from the site (or the file on your computer) it came from, the same way Chrome would, including your sign-in to that site. The PDF is only displayed, never sent anywhere else.
