# ScrollPen privacy policy

*Last updated: 6 November 2026 · applies to ScrollPen 1.0.0 and later*

ScrollPen is a free Chrome extension for drawing on web pages and PDFs. It has no servers, no
accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and it never sends your data anywhere. Its
source code is public, so anyone can check this:
https://github.com/arijit-debug/ScrollPen

## You choose whether anything is saved

The first time you open ScrollPen, it asks: **"Save your ink on this computer?"** Until you
answer, nothing is saved.

- **Save my ink on this computer:** your ink is kept so it is there when you come back to a page.
- **Don't save:** your ink exists only while the tab is open and is gone when you close it.

You can change this at any time in ScrollPen's settings (right-click the ScrollPen button →
Options → "Your data").

## What ScrollPen stores, and where

Everything is stored in Chrome's storage for ScrollPen, in your Chrome profile, on your
computer. It is not synced to your Google account or to any other device.

| What | When | How long |
|---|---|---|
| Your ink: drawings, shapes and the text you write | Only if you chose to save | Until you delete it |
| The full address of each page you draw on (including any part after "?") and its title | Only if you chose to save | Until you delete it |
| When you last drew on that page | Only if you chose to save | Until you delete it |
| Your settings: tool, colours, sizes, toolbar position, your saving choice | Always | Until you reset them or remove ScrollPen |
| The address of a PDF you chose to open in Chrome's own viewer (so the Back button works) | Only then | Until that tab closes |

ScrollPen does **not** read, collect or store the content of the pages you visit, your
browsing history in general, your clicks, keystrokes or mouse movements. It only uses the
address and title of a page you draw on, to know which ink belongs to it.

## What leaves your computer

Nothing. ScrollPen makes no network requests of its own. The only exception is showing a PDF
you open: ScrollPen's PDF viewer loads that PDF from the website (or the file on your computer)
it came from, the same way Chrome itself would, including your sign-in to that website, and
displays it on your computer. The PDF is not sent anywhere else.

## Screenshots

ScrollPen takes a screenshot of the visible part of a page only when you click its camera
button or use its screenshot shortcut. The picture is saved to your Downloads folder and
nowhere else.

## Seeing and deleting your data

In ScrollPen's settings ("Your data" and "Pages with saved ink") you can:

- see every page with saved ink, open it, or delete its ink;
- delete all saved ink;
- download everything ScrollPen has stored, as a readable file;
- turn saving off.

Removing ScrollPen from Chrome deletes everything it stored.

## Permissions ScrollPen asks for, and why

- **Read and change data on websites:** to draw on the pages you choose and to show your saved
  ink when you return to them; and to open PDFs in ScrollPen's viewer.
- **Storage / unlimited storage:** to keep your ink and settings on your computer.
- **Active tab:** to take a screenshot of the current tab when you ask for one.
- **Scripting:** to start ScrollPen in a tab that was already open before you installed it.

## Children

ScrollPen collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If ScrollPen ever changes what it stores, this policy will be updated first and the change will
be shown inside ScrollPen before it takes effect.
