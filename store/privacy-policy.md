# ScrollPen privacy policy

*Last updated: 21 October 2026*

ScrollPen is a Chrome extension for drawing on web pages and PDFs.

## What ScrollPen stores

- **Your ink** for each page you draw on, together with that page's address and title, so your ink is there again when you reopen the page.
- **Your settings**, such as colours, sizes and the toolbar's position.

All of this is stored only on your computer, inside your Chrome profile (Chrome's extension storage). It is not synced to your Google account or to any other device.

## What ScrollPen sends

Nothing. ScrollPen has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It never sends your ink, your settings or the pages you visit anywhere.

The only network requests ScrollPen makes are when you open a PDF: its PDF viewer loads that PDF from the website (or the file on your computer) it came from, just as Chrome would, so it can show it to you. The PDF is displayed on your computer and not sent anywhere else.

## What ScrollPen can see

To let you draw on any page, ScrollPen runs on the web pages you open. It does not read, collect or store the content of those pages. It only uses a page's address and title to remember which ink belongs to it.

Screenshots you take with ScrollPen are saved to your Downloads folder and nowhere else.

## Deleting your data

- Open ScrollPen's settings (right-click the ScrollPen button → Options) to see every page with saved ink and delete any or all of it.
- Removing ScrollPen from Chrome deletes all of its data.
